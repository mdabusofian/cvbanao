Bangladeshi CV Format: English Sample & Guide

Looking for a Bangladeshi CV format in English? Below is the standard structure used for most job applications in Bangladesh, with a complete sample you can adapt. Free CV maker Bangladesh: build yours here.

Last updated: October 2026 · Reading time about 6 minutes

Standard sections in order

  1. Name, applied position, contact details and photo
  2. Career objective (2 to 3 lines)
  3. Educational qualification (SSC/Dakhil, HSC/Alim, Honours/Degree, Masters)
  4. Work experience (latest first)
  5. Certifications and training
  6. Skills and languages
  7. Personal details (father's name, mother's name, date of birth, NID, religion, marital status, address)
  8. References
  9. Declaration with date and signature

Things that make a Bangladeshi CV stand out

  • Keep it to one or two pages, in a clean font and consistent date format.
  • Show results with numbers where you can, for example "reduced reporting time by 20%".
  • Write GPA or CGPA with its scale, such as "(out of 5.00)" or "(out of 4.00)".
  • Match your skills and objective to the job circular.
Note: Whether to include NID, religion, or photo depends on the job circular. Always follow the instructions given in the advertisement.
